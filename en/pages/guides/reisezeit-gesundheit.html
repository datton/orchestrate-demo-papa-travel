When to go.

This comes from travel guides, not from an authority, and is a rough guide. Exact month limits, temperatures and rainfall differ from source to source.

Dry season

Travel guides mostly name November to March. The coast near Accra stays humid all year; in the dry season it is a little cooler.

Harmattan

A dry, dusty trade wind, mainly from December to February. Weaker in the south than in the north. Early November or early December lessens the effect.

Rainy seasons in the south

There are two: roughly April to June and September to November.

Surfing

The biggest waves are given as roughly April to September (travel guides).

Source: travel guides, combined assessment. As of 21.11.2026. What suits your trip depends on what you plan to do.

Health.

  • Yellow fever: vaccination is compulsory for everyone from nine months of age, at least 10 days before the trip. The proof is valid for life.
  • Yellow fever outbreak: in the Upper West Region in the north, not in the Accra region (CDC level 2, as of 09.11.2026).
  • Malaria: there is a risk throughout Ghana. Ask a tropical medicine or travel clinic before you go.
  • To discuss with your doctor: hepatitis A, tetanus, typhoid; for some travellers hepatitis B, rabies, cholera, MMR, meningococcal ACWY.
  • Health declaration: online before you travel (compulsory since 12.11.2026); you show the QR code on arrival.
  • Medicines: some have a different legal status in Ghana. Check yours beforehand.

Source: Auswärtiges Amt, CDC, TravelHealthPro, FCDO 04.11.2026. As of 21.11.2026.

Insurance and money.

  1. Health and repatriation insurance abroad

    Recommended. Health care outside Accra is inadequate, and payment in advance is often required. Travelling against a travel warning can put your insurance cover at risk.

    Source: Government of Canada 02.11.2026, FCDO 04.11.2026. As of 21.11.2026. Ghana Vorab is fictional and does not sell insurance.

  2. Cedi, cards and cash

    The currency is the cedi (GHS). Visa is more widely accepted than Mastercard, and card and cash machine fraud is common: carry some cash. Cash over 10,000 US dollars is declared on form FX-5.

    Source: Bank of Ghana, OBG 2024, Government of Canada. As of 21.11.2026.

  3. Power and phone

    230 V, 50 Hz, socket types G and D: the German Schuko plug does not fit. There were power cuts in 2026, around six hours in parts of Greater Accra (1 to 5 November 2026). For a SIM card you need, according to an official answer, your passport and proof of entry.

    Source: socket reference (secondary source), Yen November 2026, Ministry of Communications/NCA 2026. As of 21.11.2026. Whether visitor SIMs are switched off after 30 days: please check with the providers.
