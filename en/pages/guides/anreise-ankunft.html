Landing.

As of 21.11.2026 The airport has been called Accra International Airport again since 23.02.2026 (code ACC, formerly Kotoka).

  1. 1. Done before departure

    Passport (not an ID card, valid for at least six months), visa, yellow fever vaccination certificate and the health declaration filled in online are things you should already have with you. You must show the QR code of the health declaration on arrival.

    Source: German Federal Foreign Office, as of 21.11.2026. The details are in the entry guide.

  2. 2. Keep your documents at hand

    Keep your passport, QR code and vaccination certificate where you can find them quickly at the check. A copy of your passport, kept apart from the original, is a good idea.

  3. 3. Do not take photos

    Photography is forbidden at the airport, as well as at military sites, government buildings and bridges. Source: Government of Canada, as of 02.11.2026.

From the airport to your hotel.

  • Ride-hailing: Uber, Bolt and Yango are active in Accra. Official advice is to use licensed taxis and to agree the fare beforehand.
  • Check the driver: The vehicle authority DVLA reported many forged driving licences among drivers in August 2026. Look at the driver and the car before you get in.
  • At night: Do not take a taxi alone at night.
  • Duration: From Accra to Kokrobite it is about 26 kilometres by road. Depending on traffic, you can roughly count on one to two hours. This is an estimate, please check with the embassy/source.
  • Tro-tros: You should avoid the minibuses because of the risk of accidents.

Sources: Government of Canada 02.11.2026, OBG 2024, Yen August 2026. You can book a transfer with us as a demo package; we cannot guarantee journey time or punctuality.

Money.

Currency

Cedi (GHS), 100 pesewas. Only change money at banks or licensed exchange bureaus.

Cards

Visa is more widely accepted than Mastercard. Card and ATM fraud is common: pay in cash where you can, watch out for skimmers and carry some cash.

Mobile money

It is widely used. Whether and how foreigners can use it, please check with the embassy/source.

Declare cash

Cash over 10,000 US dollars must be declared on form FX-5.

As of 21.11.2026. Sources: Bank of Ghana, OBG 2024, Government of Canada, FCDO. We give no concrete prices or exchange rates, as they change all the time.

SIM card and power.

  • SIM card: According to an official answer on SIM registration, visitors need their passport and proof of entry.
  • Network: 3G and 4G are strongest in the cities. The country code is +233.
  • Open: Whether the earlier rule of cutting off visitor SIMs after 30 days still applies, please check with the embassy/source. The same goes for roaming and eSIM.
  • Power: 230 V, 50 Hz, socket types G and D. The German plug does not fit, so bring an adapter (secondary source, please check briefly).
  • Power cuts: In November 2026 there were outages of up to six hours in parts of Greater Accra. A power bank makes sense.

As of 21.11.2026. Sources: Ministry of Communications and NCA (FAQ 2026), OBG 2024, Yen November 2026.

Your first days on the road.

  • Travel between cities in daylight where possible.
  • Robberies, pickpocketing and scams happen, and are on the rise in Accra. You should avoid Nima and Agbogbloshie at night.
  • Do not pay anything in cash at police checkpoints, and carry a copy of your passport.
  • Do not swim at beaches with dangerous currents, and heed flags and local notices.
  • Emergency numbers: 112, police 191 or 18555, ambulance 193, fire service 192.

As of 21.11.2026. Sources: German Federal Foreign Office, Government of Canada 02.11.2026, FCDO 04.11.2026. These are general notes, not a promise for your trip: check the current situation with the Federal Foreign Office.
