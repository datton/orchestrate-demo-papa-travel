The right order

  1. 1. Check your passport

    A passport, a provisional passport or a child passport is accepted. The ID card and the provisional ID card are not. Your passport must still be valid for at least six months from the day you enter.

  2. 2. Apply for a visa

    German citizens need a visa, and you apply for it before the trip. The former visa on arrival has been replaced by the e-visa: do not rely on it.

    The e-visa has been available since May 2026 on the official portal evisa.immigration.gov.gh. Or you contact the Embassy of Ghana in Berlin. Ghana Vorab does not file an application for you and cannot promise any outcome.

  3. 3. Yellow fever vaccination

    Proof is mandatory for everyone from nine months of age. Get vaccinated at least 10 days before the trip; the proof is valid for life. Malaria occurs throughout Ghana: talk to a travel clinic or tropical medicine early.

  4. 4. Fill in the health declaration

    Before you set off, fill it in online: ehds.ghs.gov.gh/declaration. It has been mandatory since 12.11.2026. You show the QR code you receive afterwards on arrival.

  5. 5. Customs and cash

    Cash over 10,000 US dollars must be declared on form FX-5. Duty-free together are spirits up to 375 ml, wine up to 750 ml and tobacco up to 0.5 kg. Camouflage clothing is forbidden.

  6. 6. Insurance and sockets

    Travel health insurance and repatriation cover are recommended: outside Accra medical care is inadequate, and you often have to pay up front. Sockets are 230 V and 50 Hz, type G and D: the German plug does not fit without an adapter.

What to have ready

  • Passport, valid for at least six months from entry
  • Visa or e-visa, still valid when you leave
  • Proof of yellow fever vaccination
  • QR code of the health declaration, saved and printed
  • A copy of your passport, kept apart from the original
  • Some cash and a Visa card

Costs and deadlines

E-visa

Tourist visa for one entry: 260 USD for holders of non-African passports, usually valid for 90 days from issue. As of 21.11.2026

Sources give different processing times. Plan generously and check the portal.

Embassy in Berlin

You apply online and submit the documents with your passport by post or in person. Processing takes ten working days, three with express. As of 21.11.2026

The embassy’s fees: please check with the embassy.

Length of stay

On entry your stay is limited to 60 days (for visas over two months). You can extend it for a fee at the Immigration Service. As of 21.11.2026

Source: Auswärtiges Amt (German Federal Foreign Office), Ghana: travel and safety advice, as of 21.11.2026. Please always check visa details on the portal or with the Embassy of Ghana in Berlin.
